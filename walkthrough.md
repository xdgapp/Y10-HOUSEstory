# HOUSEstory All Endings Walkthrough
## Asbestos Ending
1.	From the bedroom, go east to hallway.
2.	Go north to the guestroom.
3.	Either examine the cabinet or look around.
4.	PLAYER IS KILLED BY ASBESTOS
## Fall Ending
1.	From the bedroom, go east to hallway.
2.	Go north to the guestroom.
3.	Climb out of the window.
4.	PLAYER IS KILLED FROM FALLING OFF A TWO-STOREY BUILDING HEADFIRST
## Sister Ending
1.	From the bedroom, go north to the bathroom.
2.	Take a shower, then go back into the bedroom.
3.	Go east to hallway.
4.	Go east into sister’s room.
5.	PLAYER IS KILLED BY SISTER FOR TRESPASSING
## Dad Ending
1.	From the bedroom, go east to hallway.
2.	Go east into sister’s room.
3.	When sister has been killed from the player’s stench, pick up her flashlight.
4.	Go west to hallway, then downstairs to the living room
5.	Go to the door of the player’s parents’ room (south).
6.	After Mum has finished talking, use the flashlight on her.
7.	After Mum has been blinded, take the old-fashioned key.
8.	Go back north to the living room.
9.	Pull the rug off the floor and use the key to unlock the trapdoor.
10.	Go downstairs, then use the flashlight to see in the dark
11.	Pick up the house keys.
12.	Go back upstairs.
13.	Go west into the kitchen, then attempt to leave the house (west).
14.	Dad will stop the player, use the flashlight on him as you did with Mum.
15.	Since the player didn’t shower and smells, while blinded, Dad can smell the player and rams him into the wall.
16.	PLAYER IS KILLED FROM BEING RAMMED INTO THE WALL BY DAD
## Good Ending
1.	From the bedroom, go east to hallway.
2.	Go east into sister’s room.
3.	When sister has been killed from the player’s stench, pick up her flashlight.
4.	Go west to hallway, then west into the player’s bedroom, then north into the bathroom.
5.	Take a shower (so Dad can’t smell the player), then go back north into the bedroom.
6.	Go west to the hallway, then downstairs to the living room
7.	Go to the door of the player’s parents’ room (south).
8.	After Mum has finished talking, use the flashlight on her.
9.	After Mum has been blinded, take the old-fashioned key.
10.	Go back north to the living room.
11.	Pull the rug off the floor and use the key to unlock the trapdoor.
12.	Go downstairs, then use the flashlight to see in the dark
13.	Pick up the house keys.
14.	Go back upstairs.
15.	Go west into the kitchen, then attempt to leave the house (west).
16.	Dad will stop the player, use the flashlight on him as you did with Mum.
17.	Since the player had showered, Dad can’t rely on his nose to detect him.
18.	(if in standard mode) Use the key to unlock the front door.
19.	Go west, to escape the house.
20.	PLAYER HAS ESCAPED THE HOUSE, WITH NO CONSEQUENCES
